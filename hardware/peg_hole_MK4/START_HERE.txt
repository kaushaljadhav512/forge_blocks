PART DIMENSIONS
Peg: diameter 7.98; overall length 50.0; both end chamfers 0.5 x 45 degrees.
Mark a grasp line 32.0 above the bottom after printing. There is no groove.
The robot grasps with the ENDS of its fingers on this line (pad centres ~9 mm higher).
Print one peg first, verify it, then print two more for three total.

Base: plate 100 x 100 x 3; boss 24.4 x 24.4, corner radius 7;
boss height 25 above plate, total base height 28. The blind bore floor is at
Z=3 and its top is at Z=28. Total depth is 25, including the 0.5 mouth chamfer.
Straight cylindrical section ends 0.5 below the boss top.
The bore is perpendicular to the boss top. No outer boss-top chamfer is added.
Four diameter 6.5 mounting holes have centres at (+/-40, +/-40), i.e. 80 spacing.
These are OPTIONAL holes for a sacrificial mounting board, not a requirement to
drill the table. The remaining broad, flat plate can be held with clamps.

Coupon: 80 x 28 x 10, with labelled CAD diameters 9.0 / 9.1 / 9.2 / 9.3.
Blind holes are 7 deep with a 3 mm floor and a 0.5 x 45 degree mouth chamfer,
matching the base floor and mouth geometry. Labels are recessed 0.5.
A short coupon cannot verify the full 25 mm depth of the final base.

NO-DRILL MOUNTING
A. If the base is near an accessible table edge:
   Use two spaced screw-operated C-clamps or F-clamps on the outer plate.
   Each upper jaw presses on a broad protective pad over the plate; each lower
   jaw bears beneath the tabletop. Use non-marring jaw pads and distribute
   force so the 3 mm PLA flange does not crack. Keep the plate fully supported
   against the tabletop; do not bridge a gap or clamp the boss.
   Check both jaw opening (table + plate + pads) and throat depth (edge to
   contact point). Keep jaws and clamp bodies outside the full gripper path.
B. If the target is farther from the edge:
   Fasten the base to a separate rigid sacrificial board or fixture plate, then
   clamp that board to the table edge at two or more spaced points. Table
   drilling is unnecessary. Size and thickness depend on distance and loads.
   For through-bolts, recess the lower hardware so it cannot rock on or scratch
   the table. Alternatively use suitable screws into a wooden board through
   washers, making sure their tips do not protrude underneath.
   Do not leave a long flexible overhang. Check that the entire fixture cannot
   slide, twist, or lift under the expected insertion/contact forces.
Do not rely on foam tape, hook-and-loop, or hot glue as the sole constraint for
repeatable robot insertion. Attachment rigidity must be verified physically.
If a board raises the base, update the robot's hole pose accordingly. The boss
top is board-top + 28 mm. Recheck the pose after remounting.
If the underside is inaccessible, determine the table construction before
choosing a different fixture; this design does not assume a magnetic table.

SLICING — STARTING SETTINGS
Use your exact Original Prusa MK4 / 0.4 mm printer preset.
Base and coupon: start with 0.15 mm QUALITY @MK4 0.4, Generic PLA.
Use the same PLA Matte spool, temperatures, extrusion multiplier, perimeter
settings and XY compensation for coupon and base. Follow the actual spool's
temperature guidance; a brand-specific temperature is not known here.
- Base and coupon: 4 perimeters minimum, 40% infill minimum; suggested starting
  values 4 perimeters, 40% gyroid, 7 top and 7 bottom solid layers.
- Peg: change layer height to 0.10 mm, 6 perimeters, 100% rectilinear infill,
  upright with a 5 mm outer brim. This follows the source's 0.10–0.12 range.
- All parts: no supports. Keep bores and peg axis vertical. Keep scale 100%.
- Use the manufacturer's suitable first-layer defaults. Inspect the sliced
  preview for a solid bore floor and uninterrupted bore walls.
- Start without extra global XY compensation; do not combine a bore-size
  correction with an additional slicer correction unless recalibrating.
- Use a consistent seam strategy; inspect and gently remove local seam burrs.
  Do not sand the entire peg down or enlarge the bore without measuring again.
The 0.15 profile is a starting point, not a guarantee of +/-0.05 mm accuracy.

CALIBRATION AND PRINT ORDER
1. Slice and print clearance_coupon_PRINT_FIRST.stl at the base settings.
2. Separately print one peg at the finer peg settings. Remove its brim carefully.
3. Measure coupon bores just BELOW the chamfer and 5 mm below the top. The
   mouth itself is 1.0 mm wider than the cylindrical bore and is not the
   correct diameter measurement location. Pin gauges are preferable; inside
   caliper readings at this size are a screening check, not proof of accuracy.
4. Choose the CAD size whose printed cylindrical bore is 9.00 +/-0.05.
   Example only: if the hole labelled 9.2 measures 9.00, print the 9p2 base.
   This example is NOT a prediction for this printer. If none pass, adjust the
   source and recalibrate; do not select the closest failed size automatically.
5. Print ONE base_CAD_bore_*_UNCALIBRATED.stl corresponding to that result.
   UNCALIBRATED means the exported geometry has not itself been physically
   verified; it does not mean that all four bases should be printed.
6. Check the finished base bore below the chamfer, at 5 and at 20 mm depth.
   Ordinary calipers may not reach 20 mm; use suitable gauges or mark it
   unmeasured. Also measure bore depth, boss height, peg diameter and length.
7. Aim for nominal bore 9.00 and peg 7.98. Check actual diametral clearance
   (bore minus peg); the supplied acceptable band is 0.9–1.1 mm. Radial
   clearance is half the diametral clearance when concentric.
8. Check by hand for free insertion over the whole depth, with no tight spot,
   then print the remaining two pegs (and optionally one spare calibrated base).
   Verify each printed peg. Record the results in MEASUREMENTS.txt.
A PLA peg will weigh substantially less than the simulated 19 g peg. Measure
and record its mass; it should not be described as dynamically mass-matched.
The supplied README allows an 8 x 50 mm steel dowel as an alternative.

EDITING / REGENERATING
Use a CadQuery Python environment (2.7.0 was used to generate this package).
  python cad_source.py
  python cad_source.py --bore 9.16 --peg-diameter 7.98 --out custom_exports
The second command is a syntax example, not a recommended correction.
Change PLATE_SIZE or MOUNT_SPACING near the top for a custom mounting layout;
update expected bounding-box checks if changing the plate dimensions.
STEP imports retain solids but do not retain a parametric feature history.
The editable feature definitions are in cad_source.py.
